# Dust 2 Engineering Inc.

**Building permit drawings for restaurants and small commercial projects across the Greater Toronto Area** — architectural, HVAC, plumbing, sprinkler and electrical drawings stamped by an Ontario P.Eng. Flat rate **$3,500–$5,000**. Based in Mississauga, Ontario.

This account publishes **free, open tools for Ontario building-code design**: every tool ships with a live web version, a command-line version, and the underlying code tables as machine-readable JSON, so designers — and their AI agents — can use the numbers directly.

## Free Ontario code tools, by category

### Fire and life safety
- [Ontario Exit Capacity Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-exit-capacity-calculator) — exit widths from occupant load, the one-exit test, travel distances (OBC 3.4)
- [Ontario Spatial Separation Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-spatial-separation-calculator) — unprotected openings vs. limiting distance (OBC 3.2.3.1)
- [Ontario Occupant Load Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-occupant-load-calculator) — occupant load by use (OBC Table 3.1.17.1)
- [Ontario Washroom Fixture Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-washroom-fixture-calculator) — minimum water closets (OBC 3.7.4)
- [Ontario Fire Separation Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-fire-separation-calculator) — fire-resistance rating between adjoining major occupancies (OBC Table 3.1.3.1)
- [Ontario Fire Alarm & Emergency Lighting Checker](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-fire-alarm-emergency-lighting-checker) — is a fire alarm required, plus lighting levels and durations (OBC 3.2.4.1 / 3.2.7.3)

### HVAC / mechanical
- [Duct Sizer Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-duct-sizer-calculator) — CFM + velocity → round and rectangular duct sizes
- [Ontario Ventilation Outdoor Air Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-ventilation-outdoor-air-calculator) — ASHRAE 62.1 ventilation rate procedure
- [Make-Up Air Heating Calculator (Ontario)](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-makeup-air-heating-calculator) — MUA heater sizing from CFM and winter design temperature
- [Ontario Restaurant Kitchen Layout Designer](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-restaurant-kitchen-layout-designer) — drag-and-drop kitchen layouts with code-based hood sizing and equipment schedules
- [Ontario Gas Pipe Sizing Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-gas-pipe-sizing-calculator) — natural gas pipe sizing by the longest-length method (CSA B149.1 basis)
- [Ontario Combustion Air Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-combustion-air-calculator) — combustion air openings for gas appliances (CSA B149.1 Clause 8)

### Plumbing
- [Ontario Sanitary Drainage Sizing Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-sanitary-drainage-sizing-calculator) — fixture units → branch, stack and building drain sizes (OBC Part 7)
- [Ontario Storm Drainage Sizing Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-storm-drainage-sizing-calculator) — roof area × SB-1 rainfall → leaders and storm drains
- [Ontario Water Service Sizing Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-water-service-sizing-calculator) — fixture units → service and distribution sizes by pressure and length (OBC 7.6.3)
- [Ontario Grease Interceptor Sizing Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-grease-interceptor-sizing-calculator) — interceptor flow rate by the OBC 7.4.4.3.(7) formula

### Electrical
- [Ontario Electrical Service Load Estimator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-electrical-service-load-estimator) — planning-size a small commercial service
- [Voltage Drop Calculator (Ontario / CEC)](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-voltage-drop-calculator) — voltage drop vs. the CEC 3% / 5% guidelines
- [Ontario Conduit Fill Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-conduit-fill-calculator) — conductor areas vs. conduit capacity (CEC Rule 12-910)
- [Ontario Dwelling Unit Load Calculator](https://github.com/dust2engineering/ontario-dwelling-unit-load-calculator) — house service demand, the full CEC Rule 8-200 worksheet

### Structural
